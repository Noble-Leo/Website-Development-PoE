Our Story

From a small home kitchen to three branches across Johannesburg.

Sweet Heaven Bakery started in 2016 in Johannesburg. Founder John Smith began baking in his home kitchen, selling cupcakes and cookies to friends and family. Word spread quickly, and in 2018 he opened his first shop in Bedfordview. More branches followed – Fourways in 2021 and Sandton in 2024.

Today we're bigger than a typical local bakery, but we still keep it personal. We use quality ingredients and bake everything fresh every day.

Our Mission

To provide honest, high-quality cakes, pastries and sweet treats that are made with care.

Our Vision

To become Gauteng's most trusted and loved local bakery while keeping our products' quality and personal care.

Our Values

  • Honesty in every ingredient we use
  • Quality over quantity, always
  • Personal service – we know our customers by name
  • Supporting local suppliers and the community
