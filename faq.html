Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to the things people ask us most.

Do you have gluten-free or vegan options?

Yes, we do! We have a small but tasty range of gluten-free and vegan treats. Ask in store or check our menu.

How far in advance should I order a custom cake?

We recommend at least 3–5 working days. For larger cakes, please contact us earlier.

Do you deliver?

Yes, we deliver within Johannesburg for a small fee. The price depends on your location.

What payment methods do you accept?

Cash, credit/debit cards, SnapScan, and EFT for online orders.

Can you cater for corporate events?

Absolutely! We do bulk orders with custom packaging. Send us an enquiry.

What are your opening hours?

Monday–Saturday: 7am – 6pm. Sunday: 8am – 4pm. All branches follow the same hours.
